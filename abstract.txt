Abstract

The increasing volume of network traffic have created significant challenges for modern cybersecurity systems. futhermore,the rising complexity of cyberattacks makes it difficult to develop efficient tools to detect them. Signature-based intrusion detection systems (IDS) have been the standard for industries for a long time to detect cyberattacks and provide security against malicious entities. However, due to their inefficient capacity to detect certain types of threats, such as zero-day attacks, it bring up concerns on  the effectiveness of traditional IDS against increasingly sophisticated cyberattacks. The objective of this project is to build a system that is capable of detecting anomalies and malicious patterns in network traffic using technologies such as machine learning. By achieving a 99.34% detection accuracy and a 0.66% false-positive rate, the proposed Random Forest-based intrusion detection system significantly improves threat detection capabilities.This project shows that integrating artificial intelligence into network security infrastructures strongly enhances threat detection capabilities, providing a defense mechanism that overcomes the limitations of traditional, signature-based frameworks.
